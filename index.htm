          <p>De-escalation is a set of strategies used to reduce the intensity of conflict, agitation, or distress. In healthcare settings, it helps create safety, restore calm, and prevent harm without relying on physical intervention.</p>

          <div class="grid-2">

            <div class="tile">

              <h4>Why it matters</h4>

                <li>Protects patients, staff, and visitors</li>

                <li>Preserves dignity during moments of distress</li>

                <li>Supports therapeutic relationships through empathy and respect</li>

                <li>Aligns with safety and quality expectations</li>

              </ul>

            </div>

            <div class="tile">

              <h4>Mindset shift</h4>

              <p>De-escalation is not about winning an argument. It is about reducing threat, increasing trust, and keeping interactions safer and more human.</p>

        <div class="panel">

          <h3>Recognizing escalation early</h3>

          <p>Early recognition creates a chance to intervene before a situation worsens. Many tense situations build gradually and show warning signs before they peak.</p>

          <div class="accordion">

            <details open>

              <summary>Common triggers in healthcare</summary>

              <ul>

                <li>Long wait times or delays</li>

                <li>Communication breakdowns or unclear information</li>

                <li>Billing or insurance frustration</li>

                <li>Perceived disrespect, judgment, or dismissal</li>

                <li>Fear, pain, anxiety, behavioral health, or substance use factors</li>

                <li>Feeling a lack of control or choice</li>

              </ul>

            </details>

            <details>

              <summary>Early warning signs</summary>

              <ul>

                <li>Changes in tone, volume, sarcasm, or verbal hostility</li>

                <li>Restlessness, pacing, clenched fists, rigid posture</li>

                <li>Eye rolling, narrowed eyes, grinding teeth, flushed face</li>

                <li>Irritability, visible frustration, repeated questioning of limits</li>

              </ul>

            </details>

            <details>

              <summary>Why perception matters</summary>

              <p>You may not agree with why a person is upset. You do not need to agree in order to recognize that the distress is real for them. That shift often opens the door to a more solutions-focused response.</p>

            </details>

          </div>

        <div class="panel">

          <h3>Before we respond: cultural humility, bias, and power</h3>

          <p>Cultural humility is not about knowing everything about every culture. It is about staying teachable, recognizing blind spots, and responding with curiosity and respect when someone’s experience differs from your own.</p>

          <div class="grid-2">

            <div class="tile">

              <h4>What to remember</h4>

              <ul>

                <li>Patients may carry prior experiences of bias, discrimination, or disrespect</li>

                <li>Healthcare roles naturally include power differences</li>

                <li>Internal bias can influence interpretation, especially during conflict</li>

                <li>Trauma history can heighten responses to tone, posture, and language</li>

              </ul>

            </div>

            <div class="tile">

              <h4>In practice</h4>

              <ul>

                <li>Avoid assumptions about how distress “should” look</li>

                <li>Ask instead of assume</li>

                <li>Offer language access or clarification</li>

                <li>Normalize emotion without labeling a person as aggressive too quickly</li>

              </ul>

            </div>

          </div>

        </div>

          <h3>Verbal de-escalation skills</h3>

          <div class="grid-2">

            <div class="tile">

              <h4>Stay calm and controlled</h4>

              <p>Slow your breathing, lower your tone, and respond deliberately. Calm is not passive. It communicates safety and steadiness.</p>

            </div>

            <div class="tile">

              <h4>Show respect and empathy</h4>

              <p>Acknowledge feelings without endorsing unsafe behavior. People often settle once they feel heard.</p>

            </div>

            <div class="tile">

              <h4>Use simple and clear language</h4>

              <p>Stress reduces processing capacity. Use short sentences, plain language, and one step at a time.</p>

            </div>

            <div class="tile">

              <h4>Offer choices when possible</h4>

              <p>Choices help restore a sense of control. Collaboration usually beats commands.</p>

            </div>

          </div>

        </div>

        <div class="panel">

          <h3>Non-verbal awareness</h3>

          <p>How you carry yourself can either calm the room or throw lighter fluid on the tension. Distressed people are often especially alert to posture, facial expression, pacing, and eye contact.</p>

          <div class="grid-2">

            <div class="tile">

              <h4>Use an open posture</h4>

              <p>Keep hands visible, shoulders relaxed, and movements slow.</p>

            </div>

            <div class="tile">

              <h4>Use respectful eye contact</h4>

              <p>Aim for attention, not intimidation.</p>

            </div>

            <div class="tile">

              <h4>Avoid visible frustration</h4>

              <p>Sighing, eye rolling, checking your watch, or rushing can escalate the interaction.</p>

            </div>

            <div class="tile">

              <h4>Mind your distance</h4>

              <p>Physical space helps people feel less trapped and more safe.</p>

            </div>

          </div>

        </div>

  <script>

    const screens = [...document.querySelectorAll('.screen')];

    const navList = document.getElementById('navList');

    const progressFill = document.getElementById('progressFill');

    const progressText = document.getElementById('progressText');

    const progressPercent = document.getElementById('progressPercent');

    const scoreText = document.getElementById('scoreText');

    const scoreBadge = document.getElementById('scoreBadge');

    const learnerName = document.getElementById('learnerName');

    const certName = document.getElementById('certName');

    const completionDate = document.getElementById('completionDate');

 

    const storageKey = 'chcr-deescalation-progress-v1';

    let state = JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem(storageKey) || '{"completed":[],"answers":{}}');

 

    function saveState(){

      localStorage.setItem(storageKey, JSON.stringify(state));

      updateProgress();

      updateScore();

    }

 

    function buildNav(){

      navList.innerHTML='';

      screens.forEach((screen, index) => {

        const btn = document.createElement('button');

        btn.className = 'nav-btn';

        btn.innerHTML = `<strong>${index+1}. ${screen.dataset.title}</strong><span class="small">${screen.dataset.type}</span>`;

        btn.onclick = () => goTo(index);

        navList.appendChild(btn);

      });

      highlightNav(0);

    }

 

    function highlightNav(index){

      [...navList.children].forEach((btn,i)=>btn.classList.toggle('active', i===index));

    }

 

    function goTo(index){

      screens.forEach((s,i)=>s.classList.toggle('active', i===index));

      highlightNav(index);

      window.scrollTo({top:0, behavior:'smooth'});

    }

 

    function markComplete(index){

      if(!state.completed.includes(index)) state.completed.push(index);

      saveState();

    }

 

    function completeAndNext(index){

      markComplete(index);

      if(index + 1 < screens.length) goTo(index + 1);

    }

 

    function updateProgress(){

      const completable = screens.map((_,i)=>i).filter(i=>i>0);

      const completedCount = state.completed.filter(i=>completable.includes(i)).length;

      const total = completable.length;

      const pct = Math.round((completedCount/total)*100);

      progressFill.style.width = pct + '%';

      progressText.textContent = `${completedCount} of ${total} completed`;

      progressPercent.textContent = `${pct}%`;

    }

 

    function answer(btn, choiceIndex){

      const question = btn.closest('.question');

      const correct = Number(question.dataset.correct);

      const buttons = [...question.querySelectorAll('.answer-btn')];

      const feedback = question.querySelector('.feedback');

      buttons.forEach(b => b.disabled = true);

      const qId = [...document.querySelectorAll('.question')].indexOf(question);

      state.answers[qId] = choiceIndex;

 

      if(choiceIndex === correct){

        btn.classList.add('correct');

        feedback.textContent = 'Correct.';

        feedback.style.color = 'var(--success)';

      } else {

        btn.classList.add('incorrect');

        buttons[correct].classList.add('correct');

        feedback.textContent = 'Not quite. Review the principle and try again after reset if needed.';

        feedback.style.color = 'var(--danger)';

      }

      saveState();

    }

 

    function updateScore(){

      const finalQuiz = document.getElementById('finalQuiz');

      if(!finalQuiz) return;

      const questions = [...finalQuiz.querySelectorAll('.question')];

      let correctCount = 0;

      questions.forEach(q => {

        const globalIndex = [...document.querySelectorAll('.question')].indexOf(q);

        if(state.answers[globalIndex] === Number(q.dataset.correct)) correctCount++;

      });

      scoreText.textContent = `${correctCount} / ${questions.length} answered correctly`;

      if(correctCount === questions.length){

        scoreBadge.textContent = 'Excellent. You passed the final knowledge check.';

      } else if(correctCount >= 2){

        scoreBadge.textContent = 'Nice work. You are close to full mastery.';

      } else {

        scoreBadge.textContent = 'Keep reviewing the modules before completion.';

      }

    }

 

    function resetCourse(){

      localStorage.removeItem(storageKey);

      state = {completed:[], answers:{}};

      document.querySelectorAll('.answer-btn').forEach(btn => {

        btn.disabled = false;

        btn.classList.remove('correct','incorrect');

      });

      document.querySelectorAll('.feedback').forEach(f => f.textContent = '');

      saveState();

      goTo(0);

    }

 

    learnerName?.addEventListener('input', e => {

      certName.textContent = e.target.value.trim() || 'Your Name Here';

    });

 

    completionDate.textContent = new Date().toLocaleDateString(undefined, {year:'numeric', month:'long', day:'numeric'});

 

    buildNav();

    updateProgress();

    updateScore();

  </script>
